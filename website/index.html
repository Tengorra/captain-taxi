FAQ

Frequently Asked
Questions

How do I book a taxi in Saskatoon?

You can book a Captain Taxi in Saskatoon by calling 306-242-0000, using our online booking form above, or texting us via WhatsApp. We're available 24/7 — including weekends and holidays.

How do I book a taxi in Regina?

Book a Captain Taxi in Regina by calling 306-775-2222 or using our online booking form. We operate 24/7 in Regina and surrounding areas including White City and Emerald Park.

Does Captain Taxi offer flat-rate airport rides?

Yes. We offer flat-rate airport transfers to and from Saskatoon John G. Diefenbaker International Airport (YXE) and Regina International Airport (YQR). No surge pricing — the price you're quoted is the price you pay.

Does Captain Taxi operate 24 hours a day?

Yes. Captain Taxi operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year in both Saskatoon and Regina — including Christmas, New Year's Eve, and all statutory holidays.

Does Captain Taxi offer corporate accounts?

Yes. We offer corporate accounts for businesses in Saskatoon and Regina. Corporate clients receive monthly invoicing, priority dispatch, multiple authorized riders, and dedicated account management. Contact us at info@captain.taxi to set up an account.

Can I schedule a taxi ride in advance?

Absolutely. You can pre-book a ride up to 30 days in advance — ideal for early morning airport departures, medical appointments, and special events. You'll receive a driver confirmation SMS the night before.

What payment methods does Captain Taxi accept?

Captain Taxi accepts cash, Visa, Mastercard, debit, and corporate account billing. All in-vehicle card readers accept tap payments.

Is Captain Taxi licensed and insured in Saskatchewan?

Yes. All Captain Taxi drivers hold valid Saskatchewan taxi licenses and criminal record checks. All vehicles carry SGI commercial insurance and pass regular safety inspections.

How long does it take for a driver to arrive?

In core city areas, average pickup time is under 8 minutes. In outer neighbourhoods, allow 10–15 minutes. For airport pickups and pre-booked rides, your driver will be on time to the minute.
